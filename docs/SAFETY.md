# Safety and Responsible Use

NOORIX AI is an educational research prototype, not a certified assistive or medical device.

- Never present image-relative left/centre/right as physical direction, distance, obstacle clearance, or navigation advice.
- Do not advise a user to cross a road, climb stairs, or move based on a model output.
- Do not describe denomination estimation as banknote authentication.
- Communicate uncertainty and say when the system cannot recognize an input.
- Provide a clear way to stop camera capture and audio output in future interactive builds.
- Do not store camera frames or personal information by default.
- Obtain informed permission before recording or testing with people.
- Keep the user's existing mobility aids and trusted human support central; this system must not replace them.
